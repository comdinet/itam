Sync dynamic device groups reads the group list once and looks inside only the groups whose own membership rule is written against device. — the ones the portal calls Dynamic Device. That is a handful of extra calls, not one per group.
Look in every group is the thorough pass, for a group with Assigned membership that holds devices: nothing about such a group says so from the outside, so the only way to find out is to look. One call per group — narrow it with ENTRA_DEVICE_GROUP_FILTER under Entra ID if your tenant is large.
Only groups that turn out to hold devices are kept, either way.
