What you need from Entra

From the same page, copy these into .env:

ITAM_SAML_SP_BASE_URL=https://your-itam-host
ITAM_SAML_IDP_ENTITY_ID=<Microsoft Entra Identifier>
ITAM_SAML_IDP_SSO_URL=<Login URL>
ITAM_SAML_IDP_CERT=<contents of the Base64 certificate>
