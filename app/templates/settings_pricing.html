Pricing groups {{ groups|length }}

A group is a specification — model, and for Macs the memory and disk that arrive as Intune custom attributes — with a price attached. Applying it writes that price onto every asset matching the specification, so a fleet is priced by spec rather than one machine at a time.

A price can also be tied to who holds the device. A shekel price belongs to the fleet bought in Israel; the same laptop on a UK desk should keep its pounds. Exclude a group and its members' devices are left alone.
